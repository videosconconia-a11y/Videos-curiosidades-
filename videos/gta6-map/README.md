## The discovery
Leonida mirrors Florida region by region: Vice City is Miami, the Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys, and Grassrivers is the Everglades. But in the north, Rockstar placed **Mount Kalaga National Park**, a forested park "in the mountains". Real Florida's highest natural point is **Britton Hill at 345 ft**, the lowest state high point in the US. So Rockstar gave Florida a mountain it never had.
